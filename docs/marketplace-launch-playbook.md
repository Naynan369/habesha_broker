# Habesha Brokers: launch validation playbook

## Product promise

Habesha Brokers is a classifieds and lead-generation marketplace. Buyers and sellers, or customers and service providers, arrange transactions directly. The platform reviews listings against its rules and payment requirements; approval is not proof of ownership, authority, identity, condition, or professional credentials. Habesha Brokers does not act as the buyer, seller, property agent, vehicle dealer, escrow holder, or service contractor.

## Start narrow

Before opening every category nationally, choose one city and one initial category with local interviews. Compare candidate categories by: how often people need the service, how hard it is to find reliable supply, how easily a deal can be completed offline, whether listings can be verified safely, and whether sellers will pay the current publication fee. Do not treat the current €40 Stripe checkout as a launch price: it is test configuration only.

## Pilot steps

1. Interview at least 10 prospective buyers and 10 prospective sellers/providers in the candidate market. Ask about the last time they solved the problem, what they used, what went wrong, and what evidence would build trust. Avoid leading questions about whether they like the app.
2. Recruit a small, manually reviewed supply cohort. Ask each seller to provide current photos, an accurate location, a reachable contact, and evidence of authority where appropriate. Keep private identity documents out of public listings and define secure review/storage before collecting them.
3. Run a time-boxed pilot in one city/category. Make the direct-contact and moderation limits visible. Respond to every report and complaint with an owner and a recorded outcome.
4. Review weekly: approved active supply, qualified enquiries/service requests, provider response time, requests accepted, appointments proposed/kept, completed outcomes reported by both sides, reports/complaints and resolution time, repeat use, and seller willingness to pay.
5. Expand only if the cohort has enough current supply, both sides complete real interactions, support can resolve issues promptly, and sellers demonstrate willingness to pay through an actual locally compliant payment flow.

## What the current app can and cannot prove

Service requests, provider quotes, proposed appointments, and post-completion reviews provide an initial activity trail for service listings. Direct calls, WhatsApp, Telegram, and off-platform deals are not automatically confirmed by the app. Contact clicks are not proof of a completed transaction. During the pilot, ask both sides to report outcomes voluntarily; do not infer a sale from a contact click.

## Decisions before live payments

- Validate the local listing fee and payment method with sellers.
- Replace the Stripe test checkout before accepting real fees. Do not use the test €40 amount as a public Ethiopian price.
- Obtain local professional advice on payment licensing, privacy, consumer complaints, listing verification, and platform terms before launch.
- Set a written response target and escalation owner for fraud, safety, privacy, and refund complaints.
